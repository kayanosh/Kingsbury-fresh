Our story

A family greengrocer since 1987

Kingsbury Fresh started as a single stall at the local weekend market. Three generations later, we still believe in the same thing: honest produce, fair prices, and a friendly face.

We work hand-in-hand with growers within 30 miles, so your fruit and veg travels less and tastes more. No cold storage for weeks on end — just proper, seasonal food.

  • 40+local farms
  • 12khappy households
